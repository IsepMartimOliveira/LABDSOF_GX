## Objective and scope

Replace unverified comparison leads with dated, attributable evidence relevant to the target segment.

Existing drafts are inputs to review and improve; do not recreate work already supported by evidence. Preparation may overlap predecessor work; confirm dependent conclusions only after the needed findings/decisions exist.

Excluded: A full market census, unsupported compliance claims and purchase of paid trials.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Choose a bounded set of alternatives, including an informal practice and a comparable maintenance/helpdesk product; explain relevance to the proposed segment.
- [ ] Record exact URLs, access dates, product/plan/version where known, observations and evidence type for each source.
- [ ] Distinguish supplier claims, observed behaviour and unknown capabilities; do not interpret missing documentation as absence.
- [ ] Record unavailable sources, trial/demo access limits and any historical prices as limitations rather than current facts.

Reviewer opens the cited sources and checks a sample of capability claims against their references.
