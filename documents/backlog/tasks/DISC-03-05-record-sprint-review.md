## Objective and scope

Present discovery and design evidence and record the evaluator's actual feedback and its consequences.

Existing drafts are inputs to review and improve; do not recreate work already supported by evidence. Preparation may overlap predecessor work; confirm dependent conclusions only after the needed findings/decisions exist.

Excluded: An executable skeleton demonstration or claiming DEC-11 alone satisfies the product/technical revision requirement.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Prepare links to the nine applicable Sprint 1 deliverables, explaining findings, limitations, MVP size and technical trade-offs; explicitly state DEC-11.
- [ ] Record the actual review date, participants and feedback using the investor-review template, without inventing a meeting or endorsement.
- [ ] For any revised product/technical decision, record before/after, rationale and affected issues/documents; if no revision occurs, keep §5.7 open with a follow-up owner instead of manufacturing a change.
- [ ] Link sprint goal outcomes and individual contributions to real research, documents, PRs, reviews or decisions.
- [ ] Save a Sprint 1 review record when evidence exists, and reflect follow-up work in the backlog.
