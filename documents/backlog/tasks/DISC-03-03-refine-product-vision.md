## Objective and scope

Consolidate the smallest credible product experiment using the discovery evidence.

Existing drafts are inputs to review and improve; do not recreate work already supported by evidence. Preparation may overlap predecessor work; confirm dependent conclusions only after the needed findings/decisions exist.

Excluded: A working prototype, implemented UX and claiming measured impact before evaluation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Update personas, value proposition and J1–J3 with finding/source references or explicit remaining hypotheses.
- [ ] Record the MVP boundary, including a decision on IoT, optional features and the distinct approval/acceptance/confirmation/closure steps.
- [ ] Define the primary administrative-effort comparison, measurement conditions and an agreed success threshold with rationale; disclose baseline limitations.
- [ ] Review the impact-confirmation mechanism for benefit, abuse, exclusion, visibility and withdrawal/opt-out.
- [ ] Keep strategy, vision and affected requirements consistent and link the team's scope decisions.
