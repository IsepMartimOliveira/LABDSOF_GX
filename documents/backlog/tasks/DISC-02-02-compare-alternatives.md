## Objective and scope

Explain where BMC may provide value relative to alternatives, using the verified source register.

Existing drafts are inputs to review and improve; do not recreate work already supported by evidence. Preparation may overlap predecessor work; confirm dependent conclusions only after the needed findings/decisions exist.

Excluded: Declaring exclusive capabilities without evidence or automatically adding features to address competitor coverage.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Complete comparisons of users, features, strengths/weaknesses, business model, UX and privacy/trust with source IDs or explicit unknowns.
- [ ] Compare the current informal workflow as well as software; distinguish planned BMC capabilities from delivered alternatives.
- [ ] State differentiation as supported conclusions or remaining hypotheses, including whether existing products already cover the essential workflow.
- [ ] Record positioning/scope recommendations and link any available user findings; disclose where task-based product testing was unavailable.
